Principal's Message

St. Francis School, Deoghar, with all its contributions to the field of education and to the lives of thousands of students and well-wishers, stands tall peerless in gaiety and splendor. Fifty-two years of dedicated service have seen generations of protégés making success in much qualified and gentle way in their lives. The story of this alma mater is the story of every individual here.

St. Francis School has a great reputation for academic excellence and other curricular activities. It has made a niche for itself in the great arena of education in this locality. We make efforts to enkindle the dormant talents of our students and perfect them. "Let your light shine" is the motto of this school — let us work tirelessly to bring to perfection this motto in our lives.

Dear parents and guardians, you are the patrons of our school. You play a great role in building the lives of our students. Continue with your good job. Dear Teachers and students — you are the brand ambassadors of this institution. What you do, what you say, and what you achieve will speak about your alma mater.

Fr. Abraham Koladiyil TOR

Our Story

About St. Francis School

Founded by the Franciscan Brothers, St. Francis School, Deoghar (SFS) is named after the immortal St. Francis of Assisi — the greatest man born during the second millennium according to historians. Affiliated with the CBSE board, SFS offers education from Nursery through Class XII from its campus at Castairs Town, B. Deoghar, Jharkhand – 814112.

The school has a majestic multi-storeyed building with well-furnished classrooms, smart digital projectors in every room, a 1000-seat auditorium, four science labs, two computer labs, a computerized library, and sports facilities for Basketball, Volleyball, Football, Cricket, Shuttle Badminton and Athletics.
